## Heart model (`public/heart/heart.bin`, `src/heart-parts.json`)

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/

Changes made: the heart walls, chambers, valves, coronary arteries, cardiac veins and great vessels were selected from the BodyParts3D 4.0 parts, recentred on the heart, scaled from metres to decimetres, converted to 16-bit indices and packed into one binary file. Vessel centrelines were computed for the blood flow animation. The geometry itself is unchanged.

The parts were read from the packaged data in Human Atlas (https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas, MIT). No Human Atlas code is used here.
